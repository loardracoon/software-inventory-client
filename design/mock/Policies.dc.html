HOW RULES WORK
First match wins.Put narrow rules, such as unsupported versions, above broad ones.
All or any.A rule matches when all its conditions are true, or any of them, as set in the rule.
Comparisons.Text ignores case unless the rule turns case on. Versions compare number by number, so 10.2 is greater than 9.14.
Categories come from the Dictionary Hub.They say what an app is. Classes say whether your organization allows it, and they never leave this app.
Exceptions are documented.A tolerated rule needs an owner, mitigating controls, who accepted the risk and a review date (CIS 2.2 and 2.3).
Every change is versioned.Publishing creates a policy version and reclassifies all titles, so the history shows when an app changed class.
